## Description
Analyzed navigation, information hierarchy, and interaction patterns of the District app after encountering real-world usability friction when attempting to access bookings and time-sensitive updates.

## UX Intervention & Solution
- Redesigned the primary home screen structure with a dedicated four-tab bottom navigation bar: (Home, Experiences, Wishlist, Profile) to surface bookings and core journeys in 1 tap instead of deep menu hunting.
- Replaced a redundant header shortcut with a prominent Notifications entry point to support critical, time-sensitive updates (show reminders, venue guidelines, and refund statuses).
- Built a pixel-perfect, high-fidelity screen prototype in Figma adhering to strict 8pt grid spacing, auto layout, and visual contrast hierarchy.

## Outcome
- Reduced cognitive load and click depth for primary ticketing/booking workflows.
- Strengthened visual balance and established clear IA hierarchy across mobile viewport constraints.
