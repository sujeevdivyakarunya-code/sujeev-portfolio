## Description
Conducted an in-depth UX breakdown of the POP UPI app identifying a key cognitive labeling friction: the "Pay Friends" CTA caused ambiguity and hesitation for users attempting to pay non-contacts, local merchants, or service providers.

## UX Intervention & Solution
- Proposed a direct renaming refinement ("Pay Anyone") to clearly communicate universal payment functionality without requiring complex layout or architectural overhaul.
- Mapped user mental models versus system labels to demonstrate how copy precision eliminates transaction hesitation.
- Built a visual carousel case study detailing the heuristic rationale and friction-to-clarity mapping.

## Outcome
- Formulated an evidence-based copy & micro-interaction recommendation.
- Documented and shared publicly on LinkedIn as a design breakdown carousel for peer review and product feedback.
