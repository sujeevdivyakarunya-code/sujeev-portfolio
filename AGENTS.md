The `portfolio/` directory is the source of truth for identity, work, media, links, and factual claims.

Workflow:
1. Inspect the entire portfolio.
2. Determine profession, audience, strongest work, and likely goal.
3. Ask only questions whose answers cannot be reliably inferred.
4. Choose the content hierarchy and visual direction.
5. Choose the simplest suitable stack.
6. Build the site.
7. Test responsive behavior and accessibility.
8. Audit and visually review the result.
9. Fix the highest-impact issues.

Never invent clients, metrics, awards, testimonials, qualifications, experience, or results.
Never use fake portfolio work when real assets exist.
