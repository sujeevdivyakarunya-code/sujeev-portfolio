# Portfolio Design

A portfolio should communicate who the creator is, what they do, the quality of their work,
why the work matters, and what the visitor should do next.

Do not force all professions into one layout.

Video editors/filmmakers: showreel, large video previews, project context, role, clients, tools,
and verified outcomes.

Thumbnail/graphic designers: visual grids, large previews, comparisons, categories, outcomes.

Photographers: image quality, galleries, collections, fullscreen viewing, minimal UI.

Developers: products, live demos, technical contribution, stack, case studies, source links.

Illustrators/3D artists: collections, detail views, process, project narratives.

Writers/strategists: selected work, readability, context, categories, outcomes.

Premium means deliberate typography, spacing, hierarchy, restrained color, and purposeful motion.
Avoid generic gradients, excessive glassmorphism, unnecessary cards, and visual clutter.
