# AI Portfolio Workflow

1. Inspect the complete portfolio directory.
2. Identify profession, specialties, audience, strongest work, media, branding, and likely goal.
3. Ask only questions that cannot be answered reliably from the files.
4. Determine content hierarchy.
5. Choose visual direction based on the creator, audience, work, and goal.
6. Choose the simplest suitable stack.
7. Build.
8. Test desktop, tablet, and mobile.
9. Review as designer, client/employer, mobile visitor, and accessibility reviewer.
10. Fix the five highest-impact issues.
11. Check fabricated claims, broken assets, placeholders, broken links, poor hierarchy,
    inaccessible controls, and unclear CTA.
