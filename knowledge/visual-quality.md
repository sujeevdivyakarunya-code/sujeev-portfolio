# Visual Quality

Composition: clear focal point, consistent alignment, deliberate whitespace, strong rhythm.

Typography: strong hierarchy, comfortable line length, readable mobile type, controlled wrapping.

Color: accessible contrast, restrained palette, intentional accent use.

Components: every card must have a purpose; controls must look actionable.

Media: use real supplied media, preserve aspect ratios, avoid layout shifts, prioritize hero media.

Motion: short and purposeful; respect reduced-motion preferences.

Ten-second test:
Would a visitor understand what this person does and whether the work is relevant within ten
seconds? If not, improve the first screen.
